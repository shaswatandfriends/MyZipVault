FAQ

Got questions?

Do candidates need to create an account?
No. Candidates receive a unique link and complete the assessment directly in their browser. No signup, no app, no friction.
How many credits do I need?
One credit = one assessment sent. Credits are shared across your team and never expire. Start with 5 free credits — no credit card needed.
Can I customize the skill checklists?
Yes. Admins can add, edit, or remove any skill from the database through the admin panel. Changes take effect immediately.
Is the digital signature legally valid?
Yes. Each signature is timestamped and tied to the candidate's submission data. The PDF report includes all this information for your records.
What email service do you use?
We use Brevo (9,000 emails/month free) as primary, with EmailJS as automatic fallback. Admins can configure and switch providers anytime.
Can multiple recruiters share one account?
Yes. Your company gets a shared credit pool. All recruiters under your company draw from the same balance, managed by an admin.
